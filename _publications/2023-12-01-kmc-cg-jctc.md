KMC-CG recasts coarse-grained mapping as unsupervised K-means clustering on a position- and fluctuation-mode-aware embedding of residues, giving optimal CG mappings of large biomolecules. Validated on ATP-bound G-actin, the HIV-1 CA pentamer, and the Arp2/3 complex.
