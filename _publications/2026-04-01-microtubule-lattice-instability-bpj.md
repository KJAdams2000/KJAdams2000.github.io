- Analyzed terabyte-scale all-atom trajectories of ~30-million-atom microtubule lattices.
- Built a bottom-up CG tubulin model (20 sites per monomer) and ran tens-of-μs CG MD of 8- and 40-layer lattices.
- Showed that intrinsic lattice instability drives protofilament clustering at plus-end tips, and explained GTP vs. GDP differences.
