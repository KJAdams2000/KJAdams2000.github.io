SC-UCG captures multistate behavior, such as conformational switching and phase transitions, in bottom-up coarse-grained models without hand-designed collective variables.

- **Latent-variable inference.** Discrete internal states of each CG molecule are inferred self-consistently by graph message passing at simulation time, with a Bethe approximation plus AI-based inference for state–state correlations.
- **Training without iterative sampling.** Multilayer Internal State Consistency (MISC), derived from relative-entropy (KL) minimization, trains the force field without re-sampling intermediate models (under 2 min/epoch on one GPU).
- **Temperature transferability.** Trained at a single temperature (*T* = 4.3), SC-UCG places a 3D-Ising-class chiral symmetry-breaking transition (racemic to D-/L-rich) near *T*<sub>c</sub> ≈ 4.4 and recovers the collective state-switching events below *T*<sub>c</sub>.
- **Software.** Implemented as an MPI-enabled C++ LAMMPS package ([LAMMPS-UCG-dev](https://github.com/KJAdams2000/LAMMPS-UCG-dev)) with PyTorch / PyTorch Geometric training workflows ([TorchREM](https://github.com/KJAdams2000/TorchREM)).
