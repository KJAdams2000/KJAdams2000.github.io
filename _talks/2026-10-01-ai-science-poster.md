Bottom-up coarse-graining is maximum-likelihood training of an energy-based model on all-atom data. Ultra-coarse-graining (observable discrete latents) and virtual-particle coarse-graining (unobservable particle latents) are two instances of one latent-variable construction, and the framework yields a new method on each side.
